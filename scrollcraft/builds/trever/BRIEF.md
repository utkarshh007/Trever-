## 2. Grammar Selection & Why the Other Seven Lost

- **Selected Grammar:** **Architectural Hybrid (Live Blueprint + Filmic Act Choreography)**
- **Why the other seven lost:**
  - *Pure Chaptered Editorial (2.2):* Forbids interactive spotlight/magnetic closing and full-stage pinned visual peaks needed for the unfolding cube ecosystem.
  - *Pure Live Surface (2.3):* Forbids display typography and narrative brand storytelling required to introduce Trever's 12 services and company story.
  - *Continuous World / Worldflight (2.4):* Forbids document flow sections needed for the interactive Project Scope Estimator and accessible inquiry form.
  - *Typographic Poster (2.5):* Forbids the 3D/circuit visual ecosystem and lateral project rail.
  - *Gallery / Catalog (2.6):* Treats the whole site as a flat catalog without an argument or unfolding architectural peak.
  - *Split Stage (2.7):* Built for a binary A/B comparison rather than a 12-discipline software engineering company.
  - *Rhythmic Cutlist (2.8):* Forbids `pin` and `dwell`, which are essential for our unfolding Cube-to-Circuit peak.
